# C-06 — 多用途の筵・菰（AMJ Culture）

**Owner:** `sucRo-RimWorld/Ancient-Medieval-Japan-Culture`. **Current author preference (2026-10-18):** 同じ敷物を**夜は就寝用、昼は座具・食事場所**として使い、毎日の収納・運搬を不要にする。**Status:** 1.6 Vanillaの最小実ゲームPoCはPASS。専用の製品ThingDef／XML、画像、1.6／1.7製品DLLへの追加とMO対応は**未実装・未受入**。旧C-06自動収納案は[FoldableBedding.md](FoldableBedding.md)に保管するが、現在の製品実装の前提条件にはしない。

## Purpose and limits

- **中世日本の生活の抽象化:** 室内に敷く筵・菰を、睡眠専用家具でも食事専用家具でもなく、**昼夜で使う一つの生活道具**として表す。庶民が毎朝必ず敷物を片付けていた、あるいは全階層・全時代が同じ生活をしていた、と断定しない。
- **確認可能な歴史的根拠:** 『日本大百科全書』[寝具](https://kotobank.jp/word/%E5%AF%9D%E5%85%B7-81512)は、菰・筵を庶民の敷寝具として述べ、9世紀ごろの畳について**昼は円座を置いて座り夜は寝具に使った**例を記す。後者は直接「鎌倉・室町のすべての庶民が同一の筵で毎日食べて寝た」を証明するわけではない。実装は史実の忠実な生活動作再現ではなく、用途兼用の合理的な抽象化。
- **単一物体・確定寸法（2026-10-18）：** **正確に1×2マス**の同じ建造物実体が通常の寝具であり、日中は座って食事できる場所にもなる。**縦置き・横置きとも建築時の回転で設置可能**。物理占有2セル・就寝方向・表示方向・食事時の座るセル/予約範囲は回転後に整合すること。朝晩の撤去・運搬・建築・設置を必要としない。別のイスを重ねて二重配置するのを製品必須構成にしない。
- **プレイヤーに見える利益:** 別々の寝台(1×2)と食事用座具(1×1)を設置しなくても**同じ敷物の上で眠り、食事ができる**。ポーンの朝夕の余分なJob/TPSコストもない。単なる休息効率・Comfortの微量差や、未確認のRoom.Space増加を主な売りにしない。
- **住居内に据え置くことを基本**とする。持ち運べる構造であっても**毎日の自動収納は実装の必須条件から除外**。専用櫃、押し入れ、個別の寝所位置MapComponent、収納専用Job、収納先の所有者同期は、この第一次多用途機能には追加しない。旧PoCを製品へ移植しない。

| Scene | Expected effect / boundary |
| --- | --- |
| 建設・所持 | Culture独立で利用可能な**1×2敷物。縦横に回転設置でき、どちらでも睡眠と食事を行える**。Vanilla `Hay`、MO導入時は既存`DankPyon_Straw`**または**Hayが使える（AND要求禁止）。制作コストは未確定 |
| 就寝 | 通常の`Building_Bed`として元の就寝Job、休息効率・Comfort、所有者割当を使用。毎晩の配置Jobは不要 |

| **所有者専用の食事座具** | **既存ベッドの所有者割当だけを使う**。`pawn.ownership.OwnedBed == mat` かつ `mat` が通常寝具として使用可能なときのみ食事の座具候補。他人は座れない。所有者未設定では食事用の候補にならない（通常の未指定寝具としての就寝機能は維持）。追加のイス専用所有者UI／保存データは不要 |
| **通常の入植者用寝具に限定** | 専用Culture敷物は通常の入植者用ベッドとして就寝・座具兼食事を両立。通常の所有者割当は維持。**初期版では敷物の囚人用／医療用切替は非対応**。囚人・患者には既存のVanilla/MO対応寝具を使用する | 
| 時代的に違う施設 | Vanilla/MOの通常ベッド、既存Sleeping Spot、囚人・医療用ベッドの`isSittable`などは**一律に変更しない**。専用Culture ThingDefのみ座具兼用を許す |
| 部屋・心情・清潔 | `AteWithoutTable`の既存Culture 0処理、共同寝所/Greedy・Jealous、Shared Sleepingの責務を変更しない。部屋品質上昇、新しいMoodボーナス、自動洗浄、日中のレクリエーション自動追加は保証しない |

**形態の区別:** ばらばらの草・藁を床に敷くだけの**敷草**と、編んで使う**筵・菰**は別物。多用途は座って使える**編んだ敷物**を第一候補にする。草敷き寝床は追加する価値・製作容易性がMOの丸太ベッドと比較して未確定であり、この機能の実装条件とはしない。素材・休息効率/Comfortと腐敗/耐久の設定は[旧検討](FoldableBedding.md#rest-and-comfort-balance-boundary--wooden-beds-are-not-automatically-superior-2026-10-18-author-decision)を踏まえるが、新しい数値は確定しない。硬い木材にクッションがない以上、敷草/筵を丸太ベッドより必ず低Comfortと扱わない。

**MOとのコスト比較:** MOで丸太45のベッドを作りやすいという作者の実ゲーム指摘を重視する。Hayの採集歩数と収穫手間、MO Strawの脱穀による供給を評価し、**安い初期寝具**だけを訴求しない。専用座具＋寝台の**2用途を1品で代替**することを優位性とし、製作材料と作業時間を後で比較する。Hay→Strawの新しい乾燥レシピやMO必須依存は追加しない。

## Original MO bed comparison and overlap audit — 2026-10-22 source check

**Actual provider, not invented balance values:** Checked the author's original Medieval Overhaul **1.6.2.2** package (`3219596926.zip`), `1.6/Defs/ThingDefs_Buildings/Buildings_Furniture.xml`. The `DankPyon_BedLog` is **1×2**, costs **45** `DankPyon_RawWood`-category stuff, needs **500** work, `BedRestEffectiveness=0.90`, `Comfort=0.55`, and has no research prerequisite. The `DankPyon_StrawBed` is also **1×2**, costs **45 stuff plus 15 DankPyon_Straw** (both simultaneously), needs **650** work, `BedRestEffectiveness=0.95`, `Comfort=0.70`, with `DankPyon_Lumber` research prerequisite. Original Straw item is `DankPyon_Straw`, not Vanilla Hay. MO log and straw beds are *existing comparators*, **not** approved numerical templates for the new mat. The mat must earn its place through two uses in one owned object; a weaker sleep stat or cheap Hay-only cost is not automatically justified by "floor bedding".

**Source/UX distinction from furniture Mods:** The completed formal [VE/non-VE prior-art audit](MultipurposeMatPriorArt-20261019.md) remains valid: VFE basic beds and seats, Erin's futons and zabuton, and Sitting Spot's overlaid invisible seat do **not** establish the exact single-Thing bed plus owner-only seat. Additional [SocialDining (Workshop 3690966202)](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3690966202) assigns **a separate chair** in relation to the pawn's bed and adds independent social/drinking Mood Thoughts, so it is not equivalent to this mat and should not be required. [Personal Sofa](https://rimworldbase.com/personal-sofa-mod/) is a closer generic dual-purpose furniture precedent (sitting/eating and sleeping in one furniture item), but its named sofa form, the exact 1.6/1.7 loaded provider, and bed-owner-only access are not demonstrated for the AMJ period-Japanese mat; it is not proof that the current product contract is already satisfied. No third-party art or implementation is copied; no compatibility patch is approved here.

**Remaining bounded product gate:** Culture main already owns `Source/AMJ.Culture/MultipurposeMatMealSelector.cs` and its call from `TablelessChairMealPatch.cs`, including the reserved `AMJ_CultureWovenMat` ID and an `isSittable=false`/original `OwnedBed` special path. **There is still no buildable Culture mat ThingDef or accepted texture**, so those source classes alone are not a playable feature. Before promoting a product, settle a valid 1×2 four-direction visual and the actual material quantity/rest/Comfort, choose whether the initial recipe is Vanilla Hay-only or accepts optional MO Straw via an audited alternative (do not express AND ingredients as OR), and check the two-cell job/reservation and source-owned meal/bed interactions. **No numerical value, graphics, XML recipe, release or new test is authorized by this comparison.**

Original MO data are directly checked in supplied `3219596926.zip`, `1.6/Defs/ThingDefs_Buildings/Buildings_Furniture.xml`. Vanilla comparators are from [Bedroll](https://rimworldwiki.com/wiki/Bedroll), [Rest effectiveness](https://rimworldwiki.com/wiki/Rest_Effectiveness) and [Comfort](https://rimworldwiki.com/wiki/Comfort); final resolved stats in other mod configurations may differ. Comfort values are normal-quality equivalent **bases**, not claims of all constructed item qualities. Critically, existing **Comfort >=0.60** crosses the game's normal `Comfortable` Mood +4 threshold; raising .55 to .60 is therefore *not* merely an unnoticed 0.05 numerical change. This is an existing Vanilla Thought, **not** a new custom Culture buff.

**Hay has more than one acquisition route.** Vanilla [Haygrass](https://rimworldwiki.com/wiki/Haygrass) has a reference base yield of 18 Hay per mature crop plant, but **wild grasses may also yield small amounts of Hay when cut**; sowing Haygrass is not a mandatory prerequisite for building the mat. Eighteen Hay is therefore a quantity comparison with one base Haygrass yield, **not** a one-harvest construction requirement. Gathering sufficient Hay from scattered wild plants costs field labor and depends on local vegetation and availability. Under the author's ordinary MO play conditions, the **45-log bed remains easier to obtain/build first** despite that wild-grass route. Do not equate this mat with a cheap or faster first bed, and do not assign a fixed number of wild plants to 18 Hay without checking their actual loaded yields. Harvest yield varies with plant type, maturity and active Mods. Vanilla original [Straw matting](https://rimworldwiki.com/wiki/Straw_matting) consumes **2 Hay per flooring tile**; original MO **overrides that existing floor cost to `DankPyon_Straw ×2`** in `1.6/Patches/Core/Replace_TerrainDef.xml`. An 18-Hay dedicated two-cell sleeping/dining mat therefore consumes as much Hay as nine Vanilla straw-floor tiles but provides normal bedding/rest/comfort and the one assigned owner's dining seat. Do not mistake the floor material override for a second Hay+Straw ingredient.

**Previously easy-to-miss authentic MO supply:** The same supplied MO package contains `1.6/Defs/ProcessorDef/ProcessDef_DryingRacks.xml` `DankPyon_StrawProcess`: **Hay → `DankPyon_Straw`**, listed `processDays=2.5`, `capacityFactor=1.0`, `efficiency=1.0`, weather/temperature modifiers. This is an **existing upstream drying process** and should not be duplicated as an AMJ Hay→Straw recipe. It does not prove every already-owned stack can convert without a rack, suitable weather, or loaded processing dependency. MO also has original Straw stock in certain caravan traders; direct quantities and AMJ Grains optional Straw routes are separate from this candidate. It is incorrect to assume every MO Straw is a free byproduct of grain threshing.

**Balance judgment:** Compared with original MO LogBed alone, an 18-Hay woven mat trades readily obtained raw-wood construction for grass gathering and/or cultivation plus +100 build work, yielding a slightly better Comfort cap plus a second use. **LogBed remains the easier initial bed** under the author's normal MO conditions; the mat's advantage is replacing a separate assigned dining seat and bed with one footprint, not faster or cheaper early construction. Compared with a LogBed **and** original MO `DankPyon_RusticStool` (Woody ×25, work450, Comfort0.50), it replaces three occupied cells with two, saves a second furniture/build action and reduces combined work950→600. It should still be inferior to Bedroll or higher researched beds for pure sleep, and it need not duplicate the separate dining chair for any other pawn. Avoid claiming a guaranteed +4 daily mood: need must actually fill to 60%, item quality alters the effective Comfort, and alternative furnishings or food locations may be preferred.

**Rejected for this first candidate:** Hay ×36 with weak sleeping stats would greatly increase grass gathering/cultivation labor without improving dual-use utility; special wetness/rot/repair rules, automatic folding, arbitrary meal buffs, hard-required MO, turning Vanilla Hay into a new `StuffDef`, or using two `costList` entries as if they meant either/or are disproportionate technical/UX costs. A heavier 24–30-Hay candidate remains a legitimate alternative **if gameplay evidence shows dominance**, but is not the default proposed amount.

**Implementation feasibility/UX gate (not yet implemented):** The existing code only recognizes the reserved `AMJ_CultureWovenMat` ThingDef. A small optional MO material variant implemented as its **own** ThingDef (e.g. `AMJ_CultureWovenMatStraw` under an MO-detection patch) would require extending `MultipurposeMatMealSelector.OwnUsableMat` to admit *only those two exact identities*, reusing identical graphics, material-independent stats and owner-only Job conditions; it may make two near-identical menu entries. One vanilla-only Def with an extra custom C# OR-ingredient worker is not automatically simpler. Favor the smallest user-comprehensible choice after checking the original designation UI. **No source, graphic or built 1.6/1.7 DLL is changed by this balance candidate.** Treat final quantity/stat adoption, 1×2 artwork and product XML as explicit future author decisions; after those, run the established single consolidated focused original-game product acceptance and report actual assertions, not an extra research/test campaign.

## Meal-location selection — actual existing buffs, no fixed irori/table/floor ranking (2026-10-18 author decision)

**Three approved constraints:** (1) dedicated mat is exactly **1×2 cells**, (2) both North/South and East/West installation orientations work without changing bed ownership or eating behavior, and (3) when an actual valid **囲炉裏・低い食卓・床食** location competes, use the one offering the **greater existing in-game buff to the eater**. Do **not** grant the irori, a chabudai-style table, or the mat arbitrary new meal Mood stages or give one furniture class an unconditional precedence.

**What "buff" means in the current original-game and Culture:** `Toils_Ingest.FinalizeIngest` evaluates the meal location's `RoomStatDefOf.Impressiveness` for `ThoughtDefOf.AteInImpressiveDiningRoom` and can issue `AteWithoutTable` for certain standing meals. Culture already neutralizes the latter to **0**. An ordinary table doesn't inherently add a positive mood Thought just by being a table; similarly, an irori does not gain a separate meal buff merely from its label. Calculate the **existing positive room/meal Thought that actually applies at that destination** for that pawn from the final loaded Defs and current room and activity conditions. Include actual Modded buffs **only when demonstrably defined by the loaded game**, not guesses about warmth, recreation or architectural labels. The same good room may produce the same reward whether seated at a table or at one's own mat.

**Selection contract:** first filter viable and reservable normal dining candidates *within the existing Ingest chair-search radius* (table+valid seat; functioning existing irori-related eating place if supported by the active Mod; this pawn's **own** normal nonmedical/nonprison mat cell). Never invent an independent seated dining interaction for an irori or a nonexistent new chabudai. For the competing viable destinations, prioritize the **greater applicable positive mood effect**; if equal, higher obtainable seated `Comfort`; then shorter/reasonable path/reservation cost. Don't reduce the current room Thought, replace standard food Jobs, make diners traverse the entire map for a bonus, or override a hazardous/forbidden/reserved destination. Keep the old C-03 ordinary table-adjacent-chair-first path unchanged **when the special Culture mat is not one of the valid candidates**. Where it is, the old absolute table priority is specifically superseded by this higher-existing-benefit rule. This feature only changes selection for the **dedicated multiuse mat's owner**, not the seat search policy of every colonist.

**Practical uncertainty / engineering guard:** RimWorld's room Thought may be determined at ingestion *completion*, so a pre-job score is a prediction based on loaded room stats and known Thought stages, not a guarantee of all downstream effects. An irori might be warmth-producing furniture rather than a native dining seat, and a "ちゃぶ台" isn't a universal ancient-to-medieval ThingDef. Avoid inferring a bonus from the furniture name. If exact Mod-specific effects cannot be computed safely, preserve the vanilla/Culture seat-selection behavior rather than invent a score. Compute only when a pawn starts a real `Ingest` action, reuse already-found local candidates and avoid per-tick global searches. Final design can choose an equivalent bounded mechanism once target Def behavior is inspected.

**Required player-facing acceptance (not yet tested):** both physical rotations keep identical sleep+owner and sitting conditions; with two valid locations that yield demonstrably unequal real meal Mood, the owner chooses the higher one (whether that means table or their mat), and when equal selects higher Comfort then shorter path. Check the ordinary chair-only/no-mat path still preserves its accepted table preference, plus other-owned and unowned mats remain ineligible. Validate `AteInImpressiveDiningRoom` / `AteWithoutTable=0` on the exact loaded 1.6/1.7 game instead of assuming an irori buff is present. The earlier one-bed 1.6 PoC never tested rotation or best-buff competition.

## Prison/medical boundary — standard bedding only (2026-10-18 author-approved fallback)

**Author decision:** When safe compatibility with `囚人用` and `医療用` bed modes is technically burdensome, **it is acceptable and preferred to ship only the normal colonist-bed multipurpose function**. Those modes are **not product requirements and must not block implementation**. For the initial dedicated Culture mat, **do not offer functional prison or medical bed modes**; retain all existing Vanilla/MO medical/prison beds, prisoner rescue/treatment and ordinary medical-bed behavior unchanged.

**Technical correction (2026-10-18, owner-only approach):** Original `Building_Bed.ForPrisoners` and `Medical` are per-instance while `ThingDef.building.isSittable` is shared. **If the new dedicated mat declares `isSittable=true`, a Culture-only owner filter cannot prevent Vanilla's other chair-search path from choosing it.** Prefer `isSittable=false` and select only the `OwnedBed` mat through Culture's targeted Ingest redirect (see [owner-only design](#owner-only-daytime-seating--reuse-the-original-bed-assignment-2026-10-18-author-proposal)). Then a special bed mode has no permitted seated owner and there is no unconditional seat access through Vanilla. The alternative must be native-tested; do not assume a normal-bed owner check alone patches the game's default seat picker.

**Minimum product contract:**
- A single **dedicated Culture ThingDef** is a normal, owning `Building_Bed` plus a daytime sitting place; no new global patch that makes all Vanilla/MO beds sittable.
- **Owner-only seat access is mandatory, regardless of ordinary/medical/prison state**; the special modes must never inherit a dining-seat permission. If `isSittable=false` plus the owner-only Culture path is proven safe, keeping normal Vanilla bed mode toggles is an optional simplification. Otherwise prevent prisoner/medical toggles on this dedicated mat alone (standard-only fallback). Avoid consuming any occupied bed as a dining seat.
- If a RimWorld version or external Mod nevertheless attempts to switch/assign such a use, preserve safe normal bed behavior or block the incompatible conversion **without removing a prisoner/patient, duplicating a bed, corrupting ownership or causing repeat jobs**. The detailed safeguard belongs in the production implementation and the final, narrow regression check.
- **Not required for acceptance:** switching this Culture mat between normal/prison/medical modes, seated eating on prison/medical beds, or custom hospital/prison UI. Vanilla/MO furniture remains the correct choice for those roles.

**Evidence status:** One originally tested Bedroll `isSittable=true` in 1.6 Vanilla proved daytime Ingest+Comfort and later LayDown as a normal owned bed; **it did not verify any prison/medical toggle, new dedicated Def or versioned production safeguard**. The above narrows the required scope; do not add an exploratory prison/medical test matrix as a substitute for the approved normal-bed product.

## Owner-only daytime seating — reuse the original bed assignment (2026-10-18 author proposal)

**Preferred implementation, subject to final loaded-game verification:** Use the **existing normal bed owner** as the **only** dining-seat permission. No separate chair owner assignment UI, ThingComp, new identity database or extra seat object. A single pawn's `Pawn_Ownership.OwnedBed` (and equivalent `Building_Bed.owners` relationship, verified against the target game version) is the authority for both sleeping and sitting. Owner changes immediately change dining eligibility; an **unassigned mat is not an eating chair**. That does **not** disable Vanilla's ordinary unassigned-bed sleep access.

**Avoid Def-wide unowned-seat leakage:** `ThingDef.building.isSittable` is global to all instances; the previous 1.6 proof temporarily set it true on Bedroll to show the dual-use geometry is viable, but **that alone does not enforce owner-only seating**. Prefer to keep the **dedicated Culture mat's** `isSittable=false` so **Vanilla's normal table-adjacent chair selector never considers it**. Extend only Culture's existing `TablelessChairMealPatch` with a separate narrow special-case for the exact dedicated mat Def: select it as an edible-seat destination only when `pawn.ownership.OwnedBed` is that real mat, pawn is an ordinary eligible colonist, the mat is spawned/standable, not forbidden/burning/dangerous, the correct 1×2 place can be reserved and the mat is not `ForPrisoners` or `Medical`. Its ordinary owner-only sitting does **not** need `isSittable=true`; the known `Toils_Ingest` redirect sets the seat destination and real `PawnUtility.GainComfortFromCellIfPossible` uses the building at that cell. **That last mechanism must be verified in a narrow real-game product acceptance because the prior PoC tested `isSittable=true`, not this false+special-case design.** Leave the selection/behavior of all normal sittable chairs unchanged.

**Medical/prison modes with owner-only permission:** Vanilla `Building_Bed.ForPrisoners` and `Medical` change ownership and typically remove assigned bed owners; consequently, a mat in those states has no eligible owner to eat on it. If the targeted 1.6/1.7 runtime and normal reservation rules prove that condition safe, the ordinary existing **mode toggles may be retained**, but they **must never enable sitting for prisoners, patients or unrelated colonists**. This is an *implementation option*, not a requirement to support dining on special beds. The previously approved **minimum fallback remains** a standard-only dedicated mat with special-mode toggles unavailable. Do not globally disable medical/prison functions on Vanilla/MO beds.

**Existing precedent (research only, not runtime dependency):** [Get Out Of My Chair! (Workshop 3119651616)](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3119651616) provides private assigned chairs, while [This Is Mine (Continued) (Workshop 2560584629)](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2560584629) restricts other furniture by a bed's owner. These independently support the player UX concept; neither verifies the exact Culture mat source path or replaces a loaded-game check. No mandatory dependency on either Mod.

## Production and acceptance gates

1. **実装準備:** 用途兼用の1×2 Culture敷物1種類を最小単位とし、Vanillaのみでも材料・就寝・食事が成立。**ベッドの既存所有者だけが食事にも使用できる**。無主の敷物は食事座具として選ばない。専用ThingDefは `isSittable=false` を優先し、既存`TablelessChairMealPatch`に **専用Def/OwnedBed限定の座具候補**を追加する。囚人/医療用にした場合の座具機能は常に無効。特殊モード切替は安全が証明できなければ提供しない既存の縮小仕様を維持。画像と呼称は別途決定する。
2. **段階的実装:** 製品ThingDef/C#で就寝と座具の両用を実装し、MO追加資材は任意のパッチだけ。現存するCulture C-03の円座は用途の違う独立候補であり、重複した円座を自動増設しない。
3. **最後の実ゲーム受入:** **縦横2方向**で1×2の割当所有者は通常`Ingest`にて、囲炉裏/既存の食卓/自己の敷物など実際に食事利用可能な候補の**実在するバフが最大の場所**（同点ならComfort、距離）を選び、敷物を使う場合はComfort上昇と完食後に同じ物体で就寝・所有者維持。他のポーンや無主の敷物では座具として選ばず、通常の座具/立ち食いへフォールバック。普通のイスだけの場合は既存C-03の食卓優先を保つ。**医療・囚人モードでは座具として使用できず**、既存の医療/囚人ベッドは改変しない。特別モード切替は技術的に安全な場合だけ任意対応、無理なら切替不可で受入可能。実DefのVanilla材料・MO任意材料、1.6/1.7版、ゲームエラーを確認する。古い1.6 PoCを製品受入に転用しない。
4. **見送り:** 櫃・押し入れ・自動収納/運搬/再設置、`Cramped interior`のボーナス/軽減、日中の作業台兼用や娯楽設備兼用は**本段階に含まない**。その後、実際の遊びで明確な利益がある場合にだけ独立機能として再検討する。
