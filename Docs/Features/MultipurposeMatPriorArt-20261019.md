# C-06 multipurpose mat prior-art audit — 2026-10-19

## Gap and scope
Culture requires one rotatable 1x2 physical bed serving its assigned owner for ordinary LayDown and actual Ingest/Comfort, while nobody else may eat on it. No separate transparent chair, routine hauling, global isSittable change or new Mood bonus. RimWorld 1.6 Vanilla standalone; MO optional.

## Inspected alternatives
- Vanilla Bedroll and MO (vanilla_mo): Original 1.6.4871 rev598 Vanilla loaded-game proof #39183002355 established the unchanged 1x2 Bedroll isSittable=false and dual-use under test-only owner redirect. Unmodified Vanilla does not supply the user-facing dual use. MO bedside content is optional; current MO bed XML remains uninspected and is not treated as a required dependency.
- AMJ C-03 (amj): Culture main Source/AMJ.Culture/TablelessChairMealPatch.cs handles independent sittable chairs, not an owned bed-seat. Reuse its meal Job adapter rather than duplicate the normal seat path.
- Vanilla Furniture Expanded 1.6 (ve): Inspected actual public source https://github.com/Vanilla-Expanded/VanillaFurnitureExpanded/blob/main/1.6/Defs/Buildings_Furniture/Buildings_BasicBeds.xml and https://github.com/Vanilla-Expanded/VanillaFurnitureExpanded/blob/main/1.6/Defs/Buildings_Furniture/Buildings_BasicSeats.xml . It defines Bed_Simple and Bed_StoneSlab separately from Seat_Cushion and Seat_Bench (the seats have isSittable=true), not the single owner-only bed-seat. The furniture content pack also requires VEF. Under the AMJ VE rule it is prior art, not a required content dependency.
- Erin's Japanese Furniture (non_ve): https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2354938860 describes separate futon beds, zabuton cushions, tables, kotatsu, electric furnishings and shoji. The 2025 update made futons standable; no owner-only food seat capability is stated. Its latest distributed Def/XML was not available for inspection, so absence of all possible patched behaviors is not asserted. Requiring its broader content for one bed-seat is not justified.
- Sitting Spot 1.6 (non_ve): https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3236014306 places a separate invisible chair over an existing bed; it has no Comfort and does not reuse the bed owner. It violates the one-object design even where its visual overlap works.
- This Is Mine (Continued) (non_ve): https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2560584629 assigns separate furniture to bed owners. Get Out Of My Chair! https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3119651616 restricts separate chairs. Both provide ownership precedents, not one physical bed/seat; their extra interfaces and data are avoidable.

## Whole-Mod comparison and conclusion
Independent C-06 Culture integration with the existing C-03 and Pawn_Ownership.OwnedBed is justified by the missing single-Thing/owner-only behavior, while retaining useful Vanilla seats. Its narrow behavior does not add a standalone content pack or a mandatory third-party runtime dependency. VFE furniture is prior-art-only; adding an entire themed furniture pack merely to use one part incurs extra art/content/loading and balance changes. Reusing Sitting Spot adds a second Thing and loses Comfort; an external chair ownership tool adds independent UI/save policy and still needs a chair. No outside licensed XML or assets are copied.

Limitations: Sources reviewed 2026-10-19 are public 1.6 VE XML, provider descriptions, current Culture source and original Vanilla native evidence. Exact Erin/MO installed XML, 1.7 provider versions and actual irori/low-table versus mat meal bonus competition still require targeted acceptance where materially relevant. Resource quantities, product graphics, medical/prison safeguards and runtime product acceptance are separate design/implementation gates.
